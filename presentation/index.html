Section 03 · International Case Study

When Knowledge Capture Takes an Exploitative Turn

Cautionary lessons from investigative reporting: "Factory Workers Are Training AI to Replace Them… Here's the Truth" (Source: youtu.be/5BoTtw8s33g).

COLLECTION METHOD

Passive & Covert Extraction

  • Garment and manufacturing workers in India equipped with head-mounted cameras throughout entire shifts.
  • Capturing fine-motor gestures and tactile dexterity — real-world "egocentric" data impossible to synthesize in AI labs.
  • Targeting low-wage markets (~$240/month) with zero cross-border data protection barriers compared to the US or EU.
CONSEQUENCES & ETHICAL RISKS

Backlash & Erosion of Trust

  • Low-wage workers inadvertently train their own automated replacements without consent, transparency, or shared value.
  • Fosters workplace paranoia, knowledge hoarding, and active resistance across both blue-collar and engineering ranks.
  • Macroeconomic dilemma: If extreme automation displaces human workers entirely, who will have the purchasing power to consume?
"Key Takeaway: AI must never be deployed as a covert surveillance tool that destroys worker trust."

Section 05 · Comprehensive Solution

Non-Invasive Capture + Verbal Explanation Incentives

A streamlined 4-step loop transforming real-world craftsmanship into structured digital intelligence.

1

Non-Invasive Capture

Station camera records 3–5 minute natural work cycles. No change to production flow, zero disruption to cycle times.

2

Explain & Incentivize

The master pauses for 30s to explain "why rotate the part 90 degrees?" → Instantly earns professional recognition and bonus points.

3

Multimodal AI Processing

3-pass VLM pipeline (self-hosted Qwen3-VL) + Docling OCR auto-generates structured WorkProfile drafts. The expert simply reviews and approves.

4

Store & Activate

Ingested into Knowledge Graph (Neo4j) + Vector (pgvector). The expert's name is permanently linked and cited in all AI responses.

Core Differentiator of MentorMind

Experts are approvers, not document writers. Experts are rewarded for sharing know-how, never covertly exploited.
